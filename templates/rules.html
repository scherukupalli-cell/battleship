How to play

Each player hides five ships on a 10 by 10 grid: a Carrier of five squares, a Battleship of four, a Cruiser and a Submarine of three each, and a Destroyer of two. Ships run across or down and cannot overlap.

Once both fleets are placed, players take turns firing at a square on the enemy grid. The server answers with a hit or a miss, and announces when every square of a ship has been hit and it sinks.

The first player to sink the whole enemy fleet wins. You only ever see your own ships. The server keeps your opponent's board secret until the game ends.
